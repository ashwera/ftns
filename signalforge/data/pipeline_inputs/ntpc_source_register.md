# NTPC renewable pipeline source register

Review date: 18 October 2026. Company: NTPC Limited. Primary source is the official NTPC Integrated Annual Report 2024-25, checked from NTPC's publisher-hosted PDF and report tables. The report was submitted to the exchanges on 7 August 2025. No local PDF snapshot was saved, so no file hash is claimed.

The repository did not contain the named `source_catalog.template.json`. The catalog was therefore created separately at [`samaira_catalog.json`](samaira_catalog.json), with this absence documented in the JSON. The reviewed-inputs file lists seven assertions. For the requested comparison of renewable shares in parent-owned and JV/subsidiary pipelines, the four core inputs are the annual report table's two numerator/denominator pairs: 725 / 15,376 MW and 13,791 / 18,295 MW.

### Nearby reviewed claims and scope checks

- `group_installed_capacity`: 79,930 MW on consolidated/group basis and 59,413 MW standalone, printed p. 90 and repeated in §9. The source says both “commercial capacity” and “cumulative installed capacity”; it is not a renewable-only figure.
- `re_installed_capacity_group`: printed p. 220 says “Your Company has a cumulative commissioned RE capacity of 6.8 GW” as of 31 March 2025, but that sentence does not say standalone versus consolidated. The group-basis reviewed claim is only partially supported; scope remains unresolved.
- `re_target_capacity_2032`: printed p. 220 says the long-term corporate plan aims to have 60 GW renewable energy capacity by 2032. This is a target, not installed or under-implementation capacity.
- The source narrative gives exact group renewable pipeline as 14,516 MW, while another reviewed input is 14.5 GW. Parent-owned RE 725 MW + JV/subsidiary RE 13,791 MW = 14,516 MW. The exact-table parent share is 725/15,376 = **4.72%** and JV/subsidiary share is 13,791/18,295 = **75.38%**. A saved calculation's 709 MW parent numerator and 4.61% result use rounded group RE (14,500 MW), and do not match the parent-owned 725 MW row. These arithmetic checks do not assign a verdict.
- In the 24 May results call, NTPC said “currently” 33.7 GW under construction including 14.6 GW RE (PDF p. 5). In the 18 August presentation, capacity construction was 30,853 MW on 30 June and the “visible RE pipeline” was 24 GW+ (slides 6–7). These values are rounded or later dated and use different labels, so they are context and not contradictions to the 31 March table.

## Independence, conflicts, and gaps

The annual report, annual-report exchange submission, investor presentation and results transcript are all NTPC-authored or issuer-submitted disclosures. They form one source family; multiple URLs do not make them independent. The CEA reports are genuinely independent official sources, but the source pages could not be opened to the specific underlying values during this review. There is consequently no verified independent regulator match for the four March 2025 pipeline figures. The main issue is rounding (14.5 GW vs 14,516 MW), which creates a 709 MW residual instead of the table's 725 MW parent numerator, plus the need to keep group, standalone, and JV/subsidiary scope separate. Pipeline code and verification status fields were left untouched.
