# Coal India Limited reports a mix of commissioned assets and ongoing infrastructure development without explicit operational gaps.

Coal India Limited (CIL) discloses specific commissioned projects, such as a 100 MW solar plant at Patan and various rail evacuation lines. The company maintains an extensive pipeline for renewable energy (9.5 GW by FY 2029-30) and thermal expansion (e.g., 1,600 MW brownfield plant in Jharkhand). Current disclosures categorize these as under execution, development, or awarded, which precludes definitive evidence of an material gap between 'announced' and 'commissioned' capacity, as the former often refers to long-term diversification targets rather than immediate operational output.

## Counterargument

The gap between announced 9.5 GW targets and currently commissioned capacity is an intentional feature of multi-year strategic planning, where 'announced' figures represent long-term goals for FY 2029-30 rather than immediate operational failures.

## Falsification

A capacity-gap claim would be overturned by original records showing that the same projects achieved the required commissioning milestone by the last valid scheduled deadline, after accounting for revisions. Awards not yet due do not establish a shortfall.

## Limitations
