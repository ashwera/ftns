RRB NTPC Notification 2026 Out For 5,236 UG and Graduate Posts, Eligibility, Application Dates

Railway Recruitment Board is conducting the RRB NTPC 2026-27 recruitment for a total of 5,236 vacancies, including 3,548 Graduate-level and 1,688 UG posts. The online application process is open at rrbapply.gov.in. Download RRB NTPC Notification PDF, Exam Date, Apply Link, and Zonal vacancies.

RRB NTPC Recruitment 2026-27: The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has opened recruitment for 5,236 vacancies under CEN 06/2026 and CEN 07/2026 for Graduate and Undergraduate posts, respectively. The recruitment includes 3,548 Graduate-level vacancies and 1,688 Undergraduate-level vacancies across various RRBs.

The detailed notification PDFs for both recruitment categories have been released officially, and candidates can currently submit their applications at Zonal RRB website. The application deadline is 6 November 2026 for Graduate posts and 13 November 2026 for Undergraduate posts.

This article covers all the important aspects of the RRB NTPC 2026-27 recruitment, including vacancies by zone and post, eligibility criteria, key dates, selection process, exam pattern, and the complete application procedure. Candidates are advised to go through the details provided below carefully before submitting their applications.

RRB NTPC Notification 2026-27 PDF Out

The RRB has officially issued the CEN 06/2026 and CEN 07/2026 notification PDFs, detailing the recruitment for Graduate and Undergraduate positions. Graduate-level vacancies are available for posts such as Chief Commercial Cum Ticket Supervisor, Goods Train Manager, Junior Accounts Assistant Cum Typist, and Senior Clerk Cum Typist.

The Undergraduate (10+2) posts include Train Clerk, Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk (CCTC), Accounts Clerk Cum Typist, and Junior Clerk Cum Typist. Candidates should download the notification PDFs given below and go through them thoroughly to get complete information about the recruitment.

What’s New in RRB NTPC Notification in 2026?

  • RRB has removed the CBAT/CBTST stage from RRB NTPC 2026-27 recruitment, which means that there will not be any Typing Test or Aptitude Test for Graduate-level posts. The new selection process includes CBT 1, CBT 2, DV, and Medical Examination.
  • Total vacancies for graduate-level posts has been increased to 3548 from 3477 previously.
  • RRB NTPC Graduate CBT-I 2026 is tentatively scheduled for the fourth week of December 2026.
  • Recruitment for RRB Station Master post will be conducted separately from this year onwards for 1250 vacancies.

How to Apply for the RRB NTPC Application Form 2026?

Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you through the registration process:

  1. Visit your regional RRB website and click on the NTPC Application section.
  2. Select “New Registration” and provide basic details like name, mobile number, and email.
  3. Check your email for a registration ID and password. Use these credentials to log in.
  4. Complete your profile by entering your education, work experience (if any), and other relevant information.
  5. Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, and any required documents in the prescribed format.
  6. Choose a payment method and pay the application fee online.
  7. Review all the details carefully before submitting your form.
  8. Once submitted, download and save a copy of the application form, and print a hard copy for your records.​​

Graduate vs Undergraduate: What’s the Difference?

RRB NTPC recruitment is conducted for various non-technical positions in Indian Railways through two separate notifications. CEN 06/2026 covers Graduate-level posts for candidates holding a degree, while CEN 07/2026 is for Undergraduate-level posts requiring a 12th-pass qualification.

Both notifications include different posts, eligibility criteria, age limits, and application schedules. Candidates should check the requirements of the respective notification before submitting their application.
